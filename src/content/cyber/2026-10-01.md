# Breadth vs Depth

**Author**: [Ayushman Chhabra](/#/blog/author/Ayushman%20Chhabra)

**Published**: 2026-10-01

**Modified**: 2026-10-11

Should you learn breadth first or depth first?

A friend of mine wanted to get into tech/IT sales. If he were to sell a firewall, I don't think he would need to know how packet filtering works. I think he would need to study the product's competitors and what are the similarities and differences between the different firewalls. But to be a good seller, he would have to go to learn how to read people. Maybe after speaking to 100 people, one of them considers the product. After 500, another says yes but haggles on price. The 1000th customer finally says yes. In the process of speaking to 1000 people, he probably learnt a lot about human psyche and psychology.

So we have established that go depth first in at least one thing. You will gain a breadth of knowledge local to your domain which is more useful than learning breadth first. The secondary effect of that is that you develop grit, patience and a slightly more interesting personality. Just imagine going breadth first your entire life. That sounds boring and a choice I don't think I will ever make.
