# Killchain

**Author**: [Ayushman Chhabra](/#/blog/author/Ayushman%20Chhabra)

**Published**: 2026-06-01

**Modified**: 2026-06-22

Introducing [`killchain`](https://github.com/ayushmanchhabra/hacking/tree/main/tools/killchain) — a raw socket network scanner in C. It performs host discovery ICMP and stealth scanns via SYN. It has a sender and receiver thread making it fast and accurate. No libpcap, no wrappers.
